The Dogs of Chernobyl

2026  ·  AI Short Film  ·  8 min

When the Chernobyl reactor exploded in 1986, residents of Pripyat were evacuated with almost no notice. They left their pets behind. Soldiers were sent in to cull the animals, but many survived and bred in the exclusion zone. Today there are approximately 900 dogs living inside the zone. Scientists studying them have found they are genetically distinct from all other dog populations on earth — evolving rapidly in ways that are still being understood.
